# PART 1 — INSTAGRAM DM OUTREACH

## Who to target (3 tiers)

**Tier 1 — High-value partnerships** (worth the most time):

- Real estate podcasters (BiggerPockets contributors, Coffee with Carl, etc.)
- Real estate YouTubers (50k+ subs): review video opportunity, affiliate
- Real estate course creators: bundle TrueCap as a tool in their course
- Brokerage owners / team leads: bulk Pro licenses for their agents
- Real estate newsletter operators: paid placement or content swap

**Tier 2 — Mid-value** (steady, scalable):

- Instagram accounts 10-50k followers in real estate niche
- Niche podcasters: episode sponsorship
- Smaller YouTubers (5-20k): affiliate code + review
- Meetup organizers / Facebook group admins

**Tier 3 — Volume plays** (low effort, low yield each, additive):

- Smaller accounts (1-10k): affiliate program
- Active Reddit users in r/realestateinvesting (DM, never link in thread)
- TikTok creators in real estate space

Start with Tier 2 — high enough yield to matter, low enough barrier they'll actually respond. Move up to Tier 1 once you have testimonials from successful Tier 2 partnerships.

## THE KING DM TEMPLATE (copy/paste)

**The "Specific Compliment + Easy Ask" pattern.** Under 60 words. Feels 1:1. High response rate.

```
Hey {first_name} — caught your [reel/post/episode] on [specific topic they covered recently]. The point about [one specific thing] is exactly what I built TrueCap to solve.

Quick idea: would you want a free Pro account to play with? If it's useful, happy to talk about doing something together (collab, affiliate, whatever fits).

Either way, love the content.
```

### Why this works

| Element | Why |
|---|---|
| **Specific compliment** | Proves you actually consumed their content. 95% of DMs they get are templated junk — yours stands out instantly. |
| **"The point about [X] is exactly what I built TrueCap to solve"** | Bridges naturally from their content to your product. Doesn't feel like a non-sequitur. |
| **"Would you want a free Pro account"** | The ASK is something they get, not something they give. Lowers commitment to zero. |
| **"If useful, happy to talk about doing something"** | Soft, optional. They can take the free thing without obligation. Many will reciprocate anyway. |
| **"Either way, love the content"** | Closes warmly. Easy "no" makes "yes" more likely. |

## DM SENDING BEST PRACTICES

### Cadence (rate limits matter on IG)

- **Max 20 DMs per day** from a non-business-verified account
- **Max 50 DMs per day** from a verified business account
- Going over = IG flags as spam → DMs go to "requests" folder where they're never seen

### Timing

- DM Tuesday-Thursday, 9am-11am or 7pm-9pm in the recipient's time zone
- Avoid Monday morning (drowning in DMs) and Friday afternoon (checked out)

### Personalization checklist (do these BEFORE sending)

1. Did I read/watch at least 2 of their recent posts?
2. Did I reference something specific that happened in the LAST 14 DAYS?
3. Is my opening line about THEM, not about me?
4. Is my ask under 1 sentence?

### Follow-up cadence

If no reply after 4-7 days, ONE follow-up:

```
Hey {first_name} — circling back on this. No worries if it's not a fit, just wanted to make sure my note didn't get buried. Account is still here whenever you want to grab it: usetruecap.com
```

ONE follow-up only. Beyond that = annoying. Move on.

### What to track in a spreadsheet

| Date | Handle | Tier | Niche | Sent | Replied | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-06-02 | @realestatecarl | 1 | Podcast | ✓ | ✓ | Booked sponsor call |
| 2026-06-03 | @rentalqueen | 2 | IG | ✓ | — | No reply, sent follow-up 6/10 |

Aim for **10-20 DMs/week**. Reply rate at 10-20% is normal for cold DMs. Conversion to actual partnership: 1-3% of DMs.

# PART 2 — 30-DAY SOCIAL CAMPAIGN PLAYBOOK

## STRATEGY (the core thesis)

**Your edge is not posting frequency. It's anchor content.**

Most founders blast 5-7 posts/week trying to "show up." Each post gets 200 views, no one signs up, the founder burns out.

The play that actually works at your stage:

1. **3 anchor posts pinned to profile** (already built — use the ones I made)
2. **1 supporting post per week** that drives back to the anchors
3. **10-20 DM outreach per week** to potential partners
4. **Engagement comments** on 20+ peer posts per day
5. **Repurpose your 21 newsletter emails** as content fuel

That's it. Focus the energy, compound the wins.

---

## CHANNEL PRIORITY (in this order)

| Platform | Why | Time investment |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** | Real estate agents + new investors live here. Your anchor posts will work hardest here. | 60% of effort |
| **LinkedIn** | Brokerage owners + serious investors. Lower volume but higher per-conversion value. | 25% of effort |
| **Twitter/X** | Niche real estate Twitter is small but high-quality. Good for thought leadership. | 10% of effort |
| **TikTok** | Younger investor audience. Viral potential but very low conversion rate. Skip until you have momentum elsewhere. | 5% of effort |

DO NOT post on all 5 platforms. Pick Instagram + LinkedIn, do them well.

---

## WEEK-BY-WEEK PLAN

### Week 1 — Foundation (anchor posts go up)

**Monday:**
- Pin all 3 anchor posts to Instagram profile (in order: Promise → Proof → Pain)
- Update IG bio: *"The 60-second rental property analyzer. Used by 3,800+ investors. Try free → usetruecap.com"*
- Update LinkedIn headline: *"Building TrueCap — the 60-second rental property analyzer for agents + investors."*

**Tuesday-Friday:**
- Comment on 20 real estate posts/day — substantive comments, not "great post!"
- Post anchor #3 (60 seconds) to LinkedIn with the caption from your IG version
- DM 5 Tier 2 partnership targets each day

**Weekend:**
- Plan next week's supporting post (one of the 21 newsletter topics, repurposed visually)

### Week 2 — Supporting content + first partnerships

**Monday:**
- Post one Reel: 30-second walkthrough of TrueCap (paste an address, show the result)
- Cross-post to TikTok (only post if Reel is performing — don't double the work for no reason)

**Tuesday:**
- Post a carousel based on one of your newsletter topics (e.g., "5 numbers every investor deal needs")
- Use the IG post generator (`generate_batch2.py`) to make the slides

**Wednesday-Friday:**
- 20 comments/day
- DM 5 Tier 1 prospects (now that you have some social proof from Week 1 engagement)
- LinkedIn post about a real deal you analyzed (the "walkthrough" anchor narrative, rewritten as a LinkedIn post)

**Weekend:**
- Review week 1 metrics. Which post got the most saves? That's your highest-converting message.

### Week 3 — Double down on what's working

**Monday:**
- Take your best-performing post from week 2 and recreate it in 2 new formats (Reel + carousel)
- Pin a partnership testimonial to your profile if you've gotten one

**Tuesday-Friday:**
- Continue 20 comments/day
- DM follow-ups from week 1 non-responders
- Post one LinkedIn article (long-form, 500-800 words) based on a newsletter topic

**Weekend:**
- First small giveaway: "Comment '60' on this post for a free Pro month." Goal: 30+ comments → 30 trial activations → 5-8 paying conversions.

### Week 4 — Compound

**Monday-Friday:**
- Maintain the rhythm: 1 supporting post, 20 comments/day, 5 DMs/day
- Run your first IG ad: boost the highest-performing anchor post with $5-10/day, target real estate agents in your top 5 metros (PHL, KC, CLE, IND, MEM)

**Weekend:**
- Review the month's metrics. Decide what to double down on in month 2.

## CONTENT REUSE — your 21 newsletter emails ARE your content engine

Each weekly email = 5 social posts. Here's the math:

| Newsletter section | Becomes |
|---|---|
| Subject line / hook | Single-line Twitter post |
| Topic body | LinkedIn long-form post |
| 3 deal-spotter examples | IG carousel (1 deal per slide) |
| Q&A | LinkedIn post or IG Reel "answering [reader name]'s question" |
| Ship note | IG Story update (single image of the feature) |

21 newsletter emails × 5 social posts each = **105 social posts already written**. Just need to design the visuals.

For visuals, the IG post generator (`instagram-posts/generate_batch2.py`) already produces clean square posts with your brand. Spend 1 hour designing a new layout template for the carousel-from-newsletter format and you have months of content fuel.

## METRICS — what to track + what to ignore

### Track these (and optimize for them)

- **Profile clicks** — the only metric that correlates with usetruecap.com signups. Goal: 200+ profile clicks/week by end of month 1.
- **Saves on posts** — IG's strongest engagement signal. Posts that get saved get pushed in algorithm.
- **DM responses** — your partnership pipeline. Goal: 10%+ response rate on cold DMs.
- **Comments on anchor posts** — early signal of which message resonates.

### IGNORE these (vanity metrics)

- **Follower count** — doesn't convert
- **Likes** — easy to game, doesn't correlate with revenue
- **Reach** — varies wildly week to week, not actionable
- **Story views** — too high-frequency to optimize for

## CAMPAIGN BUDGET (if any)

You can run this organically with $0. If you want to accelerate:

| Spend | What it gets you |
|---|---|
| **$0** | Pin anchors + 10 DMs/day + comment engagement. Slowest but free. |
| **$50/mo** | Boost one anchor post each week ($10-15/day for 4 days). Adds ~500 profile clicks/mo. |
| **$200/mo** | Run lookalike audience ads targeting real estate agents/investors. Adds ~2,000 profile clicks/mo. |
| **$500/mo** | Above + sponsor one mid-tier newsletter or podcast episode. Adds ~5,000 reach + 200-500 direct signups depending on fit. |

For your stage: spend $0-50/mo while you validate the message. Don't scale spend until you know what content/audience combo converts. Burning $500/mo on the wrong message = wasted.

## RED FLAGS (avoid these)

- ❌ Buying followers (algorithm punishes you)
- ❌ Engagement pods (devalues real engagement)
- ❌ Mass-following / unfollowing
- ❌ Generic DMs that don't reference the recipient
- ❌ Linking to usetruecap.com in your IG bio AND every comment (algorithm flags as spammy)
- ❌ Posting daily without a strategy (burnout + diluted brand)
- ❌ Replying to negative comments with anything other than calm professionalism
- ❌ Comparing yourself to competitors who have 100x your follower count (they have years of head start)

## ONE LAST THING — REAL-TALK ABOUT EXPECTATIONS

Social media for B2B SaaS is **slow**. The 30-day plan above won't make you go viral. What it WILL do:

- Set up the systems so each post compounds
- Build a profile new visitors trust enough to click through
- Validate which messaging resonates with your audience
- Land 1-3 small partnerships that drive ongoing signups
- Add 200-500 followers (most of whom will be real, engaged investors/agents)

Real revenue from social typically shows up in months 3-6, not month 1. The work in month 1 is the foundation. Stick with it.
