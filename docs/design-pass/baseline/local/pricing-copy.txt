Frequently asked
Is this for agents, investors, or both?
+

Both, and the analyzer is the same. Agents with investor clients use Agent Pro to keep a Buy Box per client, screen each listing against it, and send a co-branded decision memo the client can open without an account and rerun with their own assumptions. Investors use the same analyzer, with Pro, for deals they are buying themselves.

Is TrueCap really free?
+

Yes. Your first decision includes asking-price cash flow, Buy Box fit, the Offer Ceiling, a downside check, and next steps. No account or card is required. Create an account to keep the work and start the free trial.

Can I cancel anytime?
+

Yes. Cancel from your profile in one click. Your Pro features stay active until the end of the period you've paid for, then automatically downgrade to Free.

How does the free trial work?
+

A new account gets 21 days to complete 3 Pro deal analyses and 1 full comparison. No card is collected, no charge is scheduled, and nothing auto-renews. If you later subscribe, checkout shows the exact charge before you confirm.

What does Pro add?
+

Pro tells you what to offer on every deal: whether it meets your Buy Box, your Offer Ceiling (your walk-away price based on your targets), what could make it fail under downside stress tests, and a report you can hand to a partner or lender, plus side-by-side comparisons. If you only need metrics, the free analyzer already gives you those.

Do I keep my saved deals if I downgrade?
+

Yes. Your saved deals and PDF exports never leave your account. On Free you'll lose the ability to edit them — and new saves cap at Free's 5-deal limit — but everything is still readable.

How accurate is the auto-fill?
+

Rent uses a HUD area benchmark (ZIP-level when available, otherwise an FMR area), not a property-specific rent comp. The rate uses FRED's national owner-occupied 30-year benchmark, not an investor lender quote. TrueCap does not auto-fill property tax from a state average or parcel source. A blank property-tax field uses a TrueCap default of 1.1% of purchase price — replace it with your local number. Replace every screening assumption with property-specific evidence before relying on the result.
