Three decisions are yours: a type set (A, B or C), a paper (Ledger, Newsprint or Bond), and approval of the ledger hero and the homepage structure. Every sample below uses the real agent-first copy from the copy pass and the real numbers the engine computes for the synthetic sample deal. Signal Blue, the semantic colors and DM Mono figures are fixed. Nothing here is built into the site yet.

Type sets

Each set is shown on the same Newsprint paper so only the letters change. Each card shows the homepage H1, the hero paragraph, a ledger block and the Pro plan card. None of these faces is in the second-generation vibe-code set (Plus Jakarta, Manrope, DM Sans, Sora, Space Grotesk, Inter, Geist) or on Impeccable's overused list; all three are open-license Google Fonts served through next/font/google.

Papers

One neutral system per paper: the page tone, a raised tone for the few objects that sit on it, the rules, a band for the row that decides, and a neutral graphite ink instead of today's blue-tinted near-black. White is kept for one job: white means write. Only the fields a person types into (the address box, editable cells in the analyzer) are white. Signal Blue stays exactly as it is today (#0066ba) and clears AA on every paper and band. Green, red and orange keep their hues and meanings; each is a little deeper so it still clears 5:1 on the band. The ledger below uses type set A.

Chrome rules

These are decided, not options. Each one maps to a grep that must come back empty at the end of the pass.

No eyebrows

No label above a heading anywhere: "For agents", "Common questions", "What Pro actually buys" and the pill-above-H1 on the persona pages all go. A heading carries its own weight. Plan names become the card's heading.

No arrow suffixes

Link text never ends in "→" and buttons carry no trailing arrow icon. A link is blue and underlined; that is enough. "See the sample deal", "For investors", "Live sample".

Sentence case, including the ledger

Labels, column heads and table heads are sentence case. Acronyms stay acronyms (DSCR, NOI, CoC). Compare below: the uppercase head does not scan faster, because the figures already differ from the labels by face and alignment. The uppercase head is also what slop-detect flags.

Cards only for objects you compare

Plan cards stay cards. Feature grids, source blocks, the FAQ and the proof blocks use rules, columns and space. No shadow at rest anywhere on marketing pages; shadows are for things that float (menus, dialogs, the sticky bar).

Radius is a hierarchy

Printed things are square; things you hold are softer.

0 · rules, tables, ledger
2 · chips, tags
4 · buttons, fields
6 · plan cards
10 · menus, dialogs

One motion, on the ledger

The double rule under the Offer Ceiling draws in once, 520ms ease-out, after the figures are already painted (no layout shift, no delay to the largest paint). Nothing else fades or slides in. prefers-reduced-motion shows the rule static. transition: all is removed from the button primitive.

The ledger as the hero

The screenshot in a rounded browser frame is replaced by the Verdict Ledger itself, as HTML. It is a server render of the real sample-deal calculation: the page calls the same engine the app calls (calculateAnalysis, calculateMaxAllowableOffer through calculateSampleDealOutcome) at build time and renders it through a new presentational ledger primitive that the app's decision summary will also use. No hand-typed figures and no image, so nothing can drift from the engine, and it adds no client JavaScript. The existing sample-deal consistency test already pins $236,000.

Homepage structure

The waterfall breaks at the top. The artifact leads, the three steps fold into one annotated walkthrough of that same ledger, and section rhythm varies with what each section holds. The FAQ keeps only questions the page does not already answer; the rest remain on /for-agents and /for-investors, where they already live.

Token strategy

Marketing and the app read one stylesheet, app/globals.css. The dashboard adds its own overrides under .dashboard-shell (lighter paper, softer rules, 14px radius, the navy rail). The plan is one system, so the change is global and reviewed on the app's key screens before rollout.

What changes, where

  • Fonts: app/layout.tsx plus the three family declarations in globals.css (root, dashboard shell, mobile sheet).
  • Paper, ink, rules, band, field and radius tokens in :root; --card becomes the raised paper, and fields get their own white token.
  • The dashboard shell stops overriding paper, rules and radius; the rail moves from navy to graphite ink (your call, listed under held decisions).
  • Shared components only: header, footer, a new Section wrapper, FaqSection, the plan card, the ledger primitive, Button (drops transition-all).
  • Out of token reach today, fixed during rollout: 70 hard-coded blue glows, 88 arbitrary shadows, the auth screen's hex colors, OG images (they load no font at all).

App screens the change touches

  • /analyze: address input, the form, the Verdict Ledger results
  • /dashboard/new: the same analyzer under the dashboard scope
  • /dashboard: home, stat cards, portfolio chart
  • /dashboard/saved-analyses and the deal workspace
  • /dashboard/compare, templates, clients, triage
  • /settings and /profile (the only users of the shadcn Card)
  • Sign-in, sign-up, password screens
  • Share viewer /s/[token] and the client portal
  • /sample-decision-memo
