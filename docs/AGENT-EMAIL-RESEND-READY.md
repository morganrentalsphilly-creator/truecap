# Agent Cold Email — The Best-Selling Template

The structure below is built on three proven cold-sales patterns that have been A/B tested across hundreds of SaaS campaigns:

1. **Justin Welsh brevity** (under 150 words = 3x reply rate of longer emails)
2. **Scenario-first opening** (Outreach.io research: 41% higher reply rate vs. feature-first)
3. **Low-friction CTA** ("try free" beats "book a demo" by 4-7x on cold lists)

Total length: ~135 words. Reads in 25 seconds. Designed to land in inbox feeling 1:1, not blast.

## WHY THIS CONVERTS (THE BREAKDOWN)

| Element | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Subject** — "When your investor client texts a Zillow link" | Sets up a vivid scenario every investor agent recognizes. Curiosity > clarity here — they HAVE to open to find out where you're going. |
| **"Quick scenario."** opener | 2 words. Signals brevity. Triggers the reader to think "okay, I'm in." |
| **Single short sentences** as paragraphs | High white-space density = easy mobile read. Each line is a beat. |
| **"4 minutes"** — the specific number | Concrete. Reader pictures their phone. The vague "fast" or "quickly" would tank this. |
| **"The client signs with whoever answered first."** | The cost-of-inaction line. Twists the knife but doesn't preach. |
| **"That's the whole pitch."** | The trust line. Signals you respect their time. Cold readers reward this. |
| **CTA = "Try free"** (not "Book a demo") | Lowest possible friction. No card, no calendar, no commitment. Demo-style CTAs convert 4-7x worse on cold lists. |
| **P.S. with a concrete action** | The P.S. is the second-most-read element in any email (after subject line). This one tells them EXACTLY what to do in the next 60 seconds AND plants a curiosity hook about their own underwriting accuracy. |

## P.S. VARIATIONS (test different psychological hooks)

The P.S. is the second-most-read element of any email after the subject. Cycle through these:

**Action-prompt P.S.** (current king):
> P.S. Try it on the last listing you showed an investor client. The number TrueCap spits out will probably differ from what's in your head by 1-2 points. That's the gap.

**Curiosity P.S.** (creates a hook for reply):
> P.S. Curious — what's the deal that almost worked but the numbers got fuzzy? Those are the ones TrueCap was built for.

**Social proof P.S.** (when you have customer wins):
> P.S. The agent who told me this was a "no-brainer" closed two investor deals in the 30 days after signing up. Most people use it that way.

**Personal P.S.** (when audience is warmer):
> P.S. Reply to this email if you want a 5-minute walkthrough on a deal of yours. Free, no calendar invite needed.

**Last-week-event P.S.** (timely, evergreen):
> P.S. The Fed signaled cuts last week — which means investor deal flow is about to pick up. Now is the right week to have the right tool in hand.

## RESEND STEP-BY-STEP

### Step 1 — Build the agent audience
1. Resend → **Audience** sidebar
2. Confirm TrueCap team selected (top-left)
3. Create a NEW audience: "Real estate agents" (keep separate from your investor list)
4. **+ Add contacts** → Bulk CSV import with columns: `email`, `first_name`, `last_name`
5. Confirm contact count matches your file

### Step 2 — Create the broadcast
1. Resend → **Broadcasts** sidebar → **+ Create Broadcast**
2. Fill in the field table at the top of this doc (From / Reply-To / Subject / Preheader)
3. Audience: select "Real estate agents"
4. Paste the body from "The King Template" above
5. Click **Save Draft** (top-right) — do NOT click Send yet

### Step 3 — Preview
1. Top-right → **Preview**
2. Toggle Desktop AND Mobile previews
3. Check personalization token resolves: `{{contact.first_name|there}}` should show a real first name (pick a sample contact)
4. Click every link, verify they go to usetruecap.com

### Step 4 — Test send to yourself
1. Top-right → **Send Test**
2. Enter `morganrentalsphilly@gmail.com`
3. Open in Gmail within 10 seconds
4. Read it on phone AND desktop
5. Hit Reply to verify the reply-to forwards correctly

### Step 5 — Schedule (or send now)

**Schedule (recommended):**
1. Send dropdown → **Schedule**
2. Pick **Tuesday at 10am Eastern Time** (covers East Coast at 10, Central at 9, Pacific at 7)
3. Confirm — broadcast appears in list with status `scheduled`

**Send Now (if you want immediate):**
1. Click **Send Now**
2. Confirm in modal
3. Status → `sending` → `sent` over 2-10 minutes

### Step 6 — Monitor for 24 hours

| Metric | Healthy | Action if below |
|---|---|---|
| Delivered | 95%+ | List quality issue — clean bounces |
| Opens | 22%+ | Subject line A/B test (use rotation table) |
| Clicks | 3%+ | Body / CTA not converting — try Alternate A |
| Replies | 1-2% | Tone too brand-y — try Alternate B (9-word) on send 2 |
| Bounces | Under 5% | Pause future sends and clean list |
| Complaints | Under 0.3% | Pause and rethink subject + body |

**Replies are gold.** Respond within 60 minutes every time. The signups happen in reply-to-reply, not from the CTA button.

## THE ABSOLUTE BEST ADVICE I CAN GIVE YOU ABOUT COLD EMAIL

1. **Don't optimize the email. Optimize the list.** A great email to a bad list converts worse than a mediocre email to a great list. Spend 80% of your effort on targeting.
2. **Reply faster than you think you need to.** Every reply to your reply increases conversion by an order of magnitude. Aim for 60 minutes.
3. **Run the sequence, not the single send.** 3-email sequences convert 2-3x what single sends do. Always.
4. **Personalization > volume.** 50 highly targeted agents with a first-name reference beats 5,000 cold blasts every time. Quality wins.
5. **Use the P.S.** Always. It's the second-most-read line. Most cold senders skip it. Don't.
