## STEP-BY-STEP — Preview, Schedule, and Send in Resend

This walks you through sending the email above to your agent list. Assumes you're logged into Resend on the TrueCap team and your domain is verified.

### Step 1 — Build the agent audience (one-time, ~5 min)

1. Resend dashboard → **Audience** (left sidebar)
2. Click the team-name dropdown at top-left → confirm you're on the **TrueCap team**
3. If your existing audience is the "General" one with your 3,852 investor contacts, **create a separate audience for agents** to keep lists isolated:
   - Top-right → **+ Add contacts** dropdown → look for an option to create/switch audiences, OR check **Audiences** in left sidebar if multiple are supported on your plan
   - If your plan only allows one audience, you can use the **Topics** feature instead: tag agents with topic `agents` and target by topic at send time
4. Once on the agent audience, click **+ Add contacts** → **Bulk import**
5. Upload your CSV with columns: `email`, `first_name`, `last_name`
6. Wait for the contact count to populate. Verify it matches the file you uploaded.

### Step 2 — Create the broadcast (~3 min)

1. Resend → **Broadcasts** (left sidebar)
2. Click **+ Create Broadcast** (top-right)
3. Fill in:
   - **From:** `TrueCap <hello@usetruecap.com>` (matches your verified sender)
   - **Reply-To:** leave blank (defaults to From, which forwards to your Gmail via the routing rule you set up)
   - **Audience:** select the agent audience (NOT the investor "General" one)
   - **Subject:** `Run any MLS listing in 60 seconds`
   - **Preheader:** `Cap rate, cash flow, DSCR, 10-year projection — paste an address, get real numbers. Branded PDF included.`
4. **Body:** Resend's editor has two modes:
   - **Plain editor** — paste the body copy from above. Markdown-style links work (`[Try free →](https://usetruecap.com)`)
   - **HTML mode** — if you want it to match the styled TrueCap weekly newsletter look, paste in HTML. (Simplest: stay with plain editor for first send.)
5. Click **Save Draft** at the top-right. Do not click Send yet.

### Step 3 — Preview (~1 min)

1. With the draft open, click **Preview** at the top-right
2. Resend renders the email exactly as it'll appear in Gmail and Outlook
3. Check on both **Desktop** and **Mobile** preview toggles — agents read on mobile more than you think
4. Verify:
   - Subject line renders fully (under 50 chars optimal)
   - Preheader shows correctly next to the subject
   - All links work (click each)
   - Personalization tokens like `{{first_name}}` resolve when you select a sample contact

### Step 4 — Send yourself a test (~1 min)

1. Top-right of the broadcast → **Send Test**
2. Enter your own email (`morganrentalsphilly@gmail.com`)
3. Hit Send
4. Within ~10 seconds, the test lands in your Gmail
5. Open it. Verify on phone AND desktop. Read it like a cold reader would.

If anything is wrong, edit the draft and re-test before moving on.

### Step 5 — Schedule (or send immediately)

**Option A — Schedule for optimal time (recommended):**

1. With the draft open, look for the **Send** button dropdown at top-right
2. Pick **Schedule** instead of "Send Now"
3. Set the time: **Tuesday at 10:00 AM in the recipient's local time zone** (Resend supports timezone-aware send for some plans — if not, use 10am Eastern as a default, which catches East Coast at 10, Central at 9, Pacific at 7)
4. Click **Schedule**
5. The broadcast appears in your Broadcasts list with status `scheduled`

You can edit or cancel a scheduled broadcast up until ~30 minutes before send time.

**Option B — Send now (if you want immediate delivery):**

1. Click **Send Now**
2. Confirm in the modal
3. Status changes to `sending` → `sent` over the next 2-10 minutes
4. Open rate + click rate populate within ~30 min and continue to climb over 24-48 hours

### Step 6 — Monitor (the next 24 hours)

Resend → Broadcasts → click the sent broadcast → see:

| Metric | What good looks like |
|---|---|
| Delivered | 95%+ of sent |
| Opens | 20%+ for a cold targeted list |
| Clicks | 3%+ of opens |
| Replies | Check your Gmail (forwarded from hello@usetruecap.com) within an hour |
| Bounces | Under 5% — if higher, your list has bad emails, pause future sends and clean |
| Complaints | Under 0.3% — if higher, the subject/body is triggering spam reactions |

**Replies are gold.** Every reply is a warm lead. Respond within 60 minutes from Gmail. Most signups happen from reply-to-reply conversations, not from the email's CTA button.

## SEND STRATEGY (THE BIGGER PICTURE)

### List size
Start with **250-500 carefully targeted agents** for the first send. Watch deliverability before scaling. The domain reputation rules are the same as the investor list — don't torch sender reputation with a blast.

### Sourcing
- Local MLS member exports (most boards allow it)
- Zillow Premier Agent directories (public)
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator filtered by "real estate agent" + your target metros
- Active commenters in real estate Facebook groups

### Cadence (3-email sequence = 2-3x more conversions than single sends)
- **Send 1** — Tuesday 10am, the email above
- **Send 2** (5-7 days later, only to non-repliers) — 60-word follow-up: *"Hey [name] — circling back on the TrueCap thing. If you've tried it, would love to hear what you thought. If not, no worries."*
- **Send 3** (10-14 days later, only to non-repliers) — *"Closing the loop. I'll stop here unless you reply. If 6 months from now you want to look at it, drop a line."*

## WHY THIS VERSION CONVERTS

- **Subject is a product promise, not a pain point.** "Run any MLS listing in 60 seconds" is the kind of subject that gets an agent to open out of pure curiosity. No setup needed.
- **Open is a 3-step recipe** (pull up, copy, paste) that's so simple it feels like the agent already knows how to do it. Friction drops to zero.
- **Bullets are outcomes, not features.** "Close more investor deals" beats "save time" every time.
- **"That's the whole pitch."** is the trust line. Confident sellers say less. Cold readers reward brevity.
- **Brand-only signature** (no founder name) keeps the email scalable. You can hand this email to a VA, a partner, or a co-founder and the tone still works.
- **P.S. is a specific action prompt**, not a recap. Reader will mentally apply the tool to their next "what do you think?" text the moment they finish reading.
