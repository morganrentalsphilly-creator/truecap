# Agent Outreach Email — Draft, Not Send Authorization

This is reviewed draft copy for a possible agent-outreach campaign. It does not
assert that a live agent audience, Resend broadcast, contact count, or send
schedule exists. Do not import contacts, create an audience, schedule a send, or
message recipients without explicit founder approval and a permissioned,
legally reviewed list-and-suppression process.

## Subject

> A preliminary screen for investor-client listings

## Preheader

## Body

```text
Hey [First Name],

When an investor client sends a rental listing, TrueCap can help organize the
first-pass calculation.

Enter a supported address and asking price, then review the labeled starting

investment advice. Replace area rent with current comparable leases, enter the
local parcel tax, obtain a property-specific insurance quote, and use written
borrower-specific financing before anyone relies on the result.

The first screen is free and requires no card. Current no-card evaluation,
paid-plan, projection, report, and branding access is shown on the live pricing
page.

Screen a property → https://usetruecap.com

— TrueCap

P.S. If you try it, compare every starting assumption with the evidence already
in your file.
```

## Proof and claim rules

- Do not say “full underwrite,” “any MLS listing,” “close more deals,” or imply
  that the screen determines whether a property is a good investment.
- Do not publish customer counts, response rates, conversion lifts, time
  savings, closed-deal outcomes, or testimonials unless the exact claim has
  dated evidence and publication approval in the proof workflow.
- Do not call the current 21-day, no-card, limited product evaluation a free
  month or subscription trial. It does not collect a card, schedule a charge,
  auto-renew, or grant team licenses.
- Projections and reports require entitled access. Custom branding requires an
  eligible paid plan. No bulk or team tier is currently offered.
- Recheck the live pricing page immediately before send; do not hard-code a
  price in outreach.

## Campaign review gate

Before any send, record approval for the audience source, lawful basis,
suppression and opt-out handling, sender identity, reply handling, subject/body,
current product facts, test recipients, schedule, volume, monitoring, and stop
conditions. Establish campaign baselines from observed data rather than copying
unverified “healthy” rates or conversion forecasts into the runbook.
