# TrueCap Marketing Playbook — Solo Execution

A draft operating rhythm for reviewed organic content and community participation. The marketing newsletter and its signup capture were canceled on 2026-07-15; nothing in this playbook authorizes restoring them.

This is built around the reality that you have a finite number of hours per week. The plan is structured so you can drop the weekly cadence to bi-weekly during busy periods and still see compounding return.

---

## The framework — three loops, one outcome

```
Loop 1 (daily, 15-30 min):    Community presence
Loop 2 (weekly, 2-3 hours):    Content creation
Loop 3 (monthly, 2 hours):     Reviewed distribution
```

Each loop can inform the next: community questions can suggest content, and reviewed content can be adapted for currently approved channels. Drafting does not authorize publication or a newsletter send.

## Loop 1 — Community presence (daily, 15-30 minutes)

The platforms where serious real estate investors actually hang out, in order of value:

1. **r/realestateinvesting** (Reddit) — ~600k members, highest density of buy-and-hold investors asking specific questions
2. **r/RealEstate** — broader, more transactional, still useful
3. **BiggerPockets forums** — the OG investor community. Still active. Higher signup friction but more buyer-intent users.
4. **r/Landlord** — operational questions, repeat tenants for blog content ideas

**The daily 15-30 min routine:**

Each morning (or whenever fits your schedule):

- Open Reddit, sort r/realestateinvesting by "new"
- Scan ~15-20 fresh posts
- Find 2-3 where you can give a genuinely helpful answer that takes 1-3 paragraphs
- Answer them. **Do not link TrueCap.** Don't even mention it. Just be the most helpful person in the thread.
- If your answer involves a specific calculation, do that calculation by hand in the comment.

**Why no link-dropping?** Because Reddit subreddits ban it within 48 hours. The play is: build comment karma + a known username (yours: `morganrentalsphilly` is recognizable + sounds like a real investor, perfect). After 3-4 months of regular helpful comments, when someone DMs you "what calculator are you using?" you mention TrueCap. The conversion rate on those DMs is 70%+; the conversion rate on link-dropped self-promo is ~0%.

**Success metric for Loop 1:** comment karma growth on r/realestateinvesting + DMs asking "what tools do you use." After 90 days you should be getting 2-5 DMs/week organically.

**Hard rule:** never reply with anything that looks AI-generated. Your edge is being a real Philadelphia investor with opinions. Lean into the personality.

## Things to NOT do

- **Don't pay influencers $X for one shout-out.** Their audience isn't going to convert without your existing brand familiarity. Get to the point of organic awareness first, then influencer collabs work.
- **Don't run podcast ads.** $$$, attribution is murky, the audience overlap with serious investors is mediocre.
- **Don't pay for SEO backlinks.** Google's spam team is aggressive in 2026. Black/grey-hat link building hurts ranking now.
- **Don't run more than one A/B test at a time on the homepage.** The instinct is to optimize fast; the reality is that traffic isn't high enough for clean statistical significance on 2+ simultaneous tests.

## Things to consider doing (later)

- **Brand affiliate program** — 25% recurring commission for the first year, fires after a user upgrades to Pro from your affiliate's link. Build when you have 100+ paid users for clean attribution.
- **Podcast appearances** — being a guest on real estate podcasts (BiggerPockets, Coach Carson, The Bigger Pockets Money Show). Lower-friction than running ads; you get the audience without paying for it.

- **Conference booth** at a small market-specific event. Not BPCon (too expensive for the lead quality at your stage). Look for state apartment association meetups or local REIA chapter events.
