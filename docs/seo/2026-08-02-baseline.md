## The uncomfortable reading of that table

The site has ~413 indexable URLs, 69 long-form posts, 20 calculators, 40
comparison pages, 34 glossary terms, clean canonicals, breadcrumbs everywhere,
and an `llms.txt`. The technical foundation is better than most production
sites. And it ranks for approximately nothing.

That combination says the binding constraint is **not content volume**. It is
authority and corroboration. Publishing faster into a 413-page site that
ranks for zero head terms adds pages to the same pile. The two findings below
are, on this evidence, worth more than the next twenty blog posts.

## Two things to fix by hand

### 1. Zero third-party mentions

Searched `usetruecap`, `"TrueCap" rental calculator`, Reddit-scoped and
listicle-scoped phrasings, and fetched the obvious roundups directly. **Not one
third-party page mentions TrueCap.**

Pages that should list it and don't:

- <https://alternativeto.net/software/dealcheck> — lists RentFlow, SpreadRun,
  CapScout, becvio, The Invest Nest
- <https://realestatestackhub.com/compare/rental-property-analysis-tools> —
  lists Stessa, RentRedi, DoorLoop, Baselane, Avail, TenantCloud, Buildium

Smaller and newer tools got themselves onto those pages. This is a submission
gap, not an authority gap — and it's the substrate AI assistants read when
asked to recommend a tool, which is why "TrueCap" is structurally
unrecommendable right now regardless of product quality.

Also worth knowing: **the brand name collides** with truecapcre.com,
true-cap.com, truecap.io, and TrueCap Advisory Services. Bare "TrueCap" is not
a winnable brand query. Use "TrueCap (usetruecap.com)" in every off-domain
placement so mentions consolidate.

### 2. The staging deployment was cannibalising the brand

`truecap-iota.vercel.app` was live, indexed, serving `robots: index, follow`
with a **self-referencing canonical to the vercel.app URL** — and outranking
usetruecap.com on brand queries. A complete duplicate of the site competing
with the real domain.

Fixed in this PR: `proxy.ts` now stamps `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow` on
any response served from a hostname that isn't usetruecap.com
(`isCanonicalHost` in `lib/site-url.ts`). Header rather than meta tag so it
also covers `/sitemap.xml`, `/robots.txt`, `/feed.xml`, `/llms.txt`, and the OG
image routes.

The weekly health check asserts the guard is still live, so this can't quietly
come back.

## Technical audit — what the codebase scan found

413 sitemap URLs across 189 `page.tsx` files.

### Fixed in this PR

| Finding | Fix |
|---|---|
| `&apos;` served literally in 7 blog meta descriptions | real characters; hard CI gate added |
| `llms-full.txt` listed 14 of 20 calculators — including the cash-flow calculator, a primary ranking target | derived from `CALCULATOR_REGISTRY`; parity test added |
| `/compare`, `/saved-analyses`, `/templates` returned 307 (temporary) | `permanentRedirect` → 308 |
| `*.vercel.app` aliases fully indexable | host guard in `proxy.ts` |

### Recorded as debt in `guard-baseline.json`

| Finding | Scale |
|---|---|
| posts below the internal-linking standard (SEO-ROADMAP §8) | **68 / 69** |
| posts with zero `/markets` links | 65 / 69 |
| posts with zero `/glossary` links | 47 / 69 |
| meta descriptions over 165 chars | 45 (longest: 301) |
| blog posts with no FAQPage | 7 / 69 |
| indexable titles over the 60-char SERP window | 5 |

The five three-way comparison posts (`dealcheck-vs-biggerpockets-vs-truecap`
and siblings) are complete link dead-ends — 0/0/0/0 — while sitting at sitemap
priority 0.8–0.9.

### Known, not yet addressed

- **`RelatedBlogPosts` shows the same 3 newest posts on all 69 posts.**
  `components/marketing/related-blog-posts.tsx` slices a newest-first list, so
  the 3 most recent posts collect ~69 internal links each and everything else
  gets none from that surface — despite `lib/blog-topics.ts` already mapping 68
  posts to 8 topics. Fixing this is probably the cheapest ranking lift in the
  repo.
- **150 programmatic `/markets/<city>` pages** are 39% of the sitemap at a
  median 43 words of unique prose, and 102 of them have no SAFMR data so the
  ZIP-level rent table renders empty. Whether this trips scaled-content policy
  is a judgment call; the mechanical proxy is the GSC indexed ratio for
  `/markets/*`.
- **Topic hubs are one-directional** — hubs link to posts, only 3 of 69 posts
  link back.
- **`/glossary` is not in the site footer**, so 34 term pages depend on a
  single index page for link flow. That is the structural cause of the
  glossary column above.
- **5 persona pages (`/for-*`) ship zero JSON-LD.**
- **`/why-truecap` has no `<h1>`** and duplicates the homepage FAQ block —
  same `FAQPage` schema on two indexable URLs.
- **`llms.txt` omits all 162 `/markets` URLs** and lists 7 of 40 `/vs` pages.
- **`app/sitemap.ts` imports `BLOG_POSTS` from `app/blog/page.tsx`**, dragging
  the component tree into the sitemap's module graph. Lifting it to
  `lib/blog-posts.ts` would let the guards assert against the real sitemap
  output instead of re-deriving it.

### What's genuinely solid

Not everything needs work, and the guards shouldn't spend budget re-litigating
these: canonical hygiene (186/189, all self-referential), `noindex` discipline
(19 correct declarations), `robots.ts`, breadcrumbs (69/69 posts, 21/21 tools,
40/40 vs), heading structure across the blog (exactly one h1, no skipped
levels), and genuine content uniqueness on `/tools` and `/vs` — zero long
sentences repeated across half or more of those pages.

## Competitors and their edge

| Domain | Edge |
|---|---|
| biggerpockets.com | community moat; forum threads rank on their own, huge backlink profile |
| dealcheck.io | category brand; owns the comparison layer, competitors' "alternative" pages funnel authority back to it |
| calculator.net | raw domain authority on a generic utility domain, near-zero content depth |
| omnicalculator.com | templated calculators at scale, each with formula explainer + schema |
| sparkrental / walletburst | free-tool spread into the niche terms the big domains ignore — the exact lane TrueCap wants |
| turbotenant / baselane / stessa | adjacent-SaaS content flywheel capturing informational queries upstream of the tool query |
| alternativeto / g2 / softwareadvice | the aggregator layer AI assistants read; inclusion is a submission decision, not a ranking contest |
