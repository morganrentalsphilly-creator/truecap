## Part D — this week's single highest-leverage action

**Target the two free-tier aggregator submission doors that are still open: AlternativeTo (DealCheck page) and the PropLab "best free underwriting 2026" roundup.**

Evidence: AlternativeTo lists 5 alternatives to DealCheck (RentFlow, SpreadRun, CapScout, becvio, The Invest Nest) — all of which are roughly TrueCap's size class, zero of them larger. The mere presence of 5 comps at that scale proves the listing filter is not "must have 10M ARR." PropLab lists exactly 10 tools; Deal Run lists 5. Both are editorial gatekeeping at the article level, not algorithmic ranking. A submission gets reviewed for fit (free tier? underwriting-capable? designed for house-hackers or small-portfolio investors?), and TrueCap checks every box all five of those comps check.

This is higher-leverage than the third-party mentions hunt (Part B of the outreach doc) because aggregator submissions are decided editors, not cold emails. It is the exact motion that is already working for smaller competitors.
